Chapter 1: The Beginning

Maria had always believed that the old lighthouse held secrets. Every summer she would walk the cliff path just to look at it, wondering who had lived there and what they had seen from that tower.

Her grandfather used to say the lighthouse keeper vanished one stormy night, leaving nothing behind but a half-written letter. No one ever found out who he was writing to.

Chapter 2: The Letter

Years later, Maria found herself standing at the lighthouse door with a key she didn't remember owning. It felt strange, almost like the building had been waiting.

Inside, dust covered everything except a small wooden desk near the window. On it sat the very letter her grandfather had spoken of, yellowed but intact.

She read the first line and felt her hands go cold.
